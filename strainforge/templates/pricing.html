Questions

Do I need an account to use the calculators?

No. Every calculator and tool works without signing in. An account is only needed to save results, file them in projects, download reports or add your own materials.

What does early access mean?

Billing isn't switched on yet, so every Personal and Team feature is free for now. We'll let you know before anything is charged.

How is a team priced?

$49.99 a month covers 3 engineers, the admin among them. Each extra engineer is $14.99 a month. Paying yearly gives you one month free: $549 a year for the team, plus $164 a year for each extra engineer.

What's in a report?

The drawings, key results, design checks, every input, the design values, each calculation step and the equations used, in the units you have chosen. The PDF fits on one or two pages; the Excel file keeps the numbers as numbers so you can work with them.

What is a project?

A folder for calculations that belong together, such as one part or one customer job. You can file calculations from any calculator in it, open them from one place and download the whole project as one PDF or one Excel workbook. In a team, everyone sees the same projects.
