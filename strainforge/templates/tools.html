About Strainforge

Strainforge is a free set of engineering calculators for cold forming and cold heading of fasteners and formed parts. The forming load and energy calculator covers cut off, upsetting, forward and backward extrusion, piercing and trimming, with flow curves for common cold heading steels and non-ferrous wires. The tool design calculators size slotted sleeve die assemblies (shrink fit, contact pressure, press-in force) and disc spring stacks (load, stress and fatigue life). Results work in metric or imperial units, and you can save, share and export them with a free account.
